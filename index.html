Questions

Good things to know before a shoot.

A quick guide to how content days usually work, what you receive, and how the final videos can be used.

How quickly are videos delivered?

Most projects are planned with a clear delivery window before filming, with priority edits available for launches and events.

Do you film on location?

Yes. Content is filmed in your restaurant, venue, event space, or brand setting so it feels true to the experience.

Can the content be used for ads?

Yes. Content can be shaped for organic social, paid social, launches, website use, and campaign assets.

Do you help with ideas?

Yes. Shoots include planning around key moments, hooks, formats, and the content your audience is most likely to respond to.
